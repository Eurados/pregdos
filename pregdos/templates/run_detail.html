DoseToWater is reported as physical absorbed dose in Gy; PregDos does not apply proton RBE to this quantity. Clinical Eclipse proton RTDOSE values are commonly stored as Gy(RBE), so compare those separately from physical DoseToWater.

{% if plan_fractions %} Doses are scaled from the simulated histories to the planned treatment course (PARTICLE_SCALING × REQUESTED_HISTORIES / Σ spotWeight × fractions). {% else %} Doses are scaled from the simulated histories to the generated TOPAS plan scale (PARTICLE_SCALING × REQUESTED_HISTORIES / Σ spotWeight). {% endif %} The uncertainty is the 1σ Monte-Carlo statistical error, √N · SD / Sum (N = simulated histories), scaled by the same factor — the plain TOPAS Standard_Deviation is the per-history spread and is √N too small as an error on the reported dose.

Single-bin structure rows are normalized with the TOPAS RTSTRUCT mask pre-pass, which gives the structure mass and volume from the selected CT HU-density table. Energy rows (EnergyDeposit) are converted to Gy by energy / structure mass (mass-normalized). Intensive dose and fluence rows (DoseToWater, H*(10)) are divided by the whole patient box by TOPAS, so PregDos rescales them from that scorer-bin volume to the structure volume — × Vpatient / Vstructure (volume-normalized). Absolute doses remain under validation until issue #50 is closed.
